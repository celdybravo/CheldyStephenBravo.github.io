Robotics Arm

This section contains a chronological order of the improvements of a robot arm, which I started with much cheaper materials and simpler design.

First Design

At the time I made this, I had very limited number of electrical components. I only had 1 micro servo motor and 1 3V DC motor

I used an Arduino UNO. This was the first time I studied C++.

I had trouble controlling the DC motor due to maybe the pins not delivering enough voltage so I used a button and unnecessarily added a transistor just to explore. I then added a toothpick to add a limit to where the DC motor will spin to.

Second Design

In this design, I tried to lighten the weight of the frame. I then used a rubber band to allow the DC motor to return to its original place. Unfortunately, the band was too strong for the DC motor to actually move. Since the robot was too tall, it frequently fell over.

Third Design

This was my latest improvement. This time, I had 2 servo motors and also a new material for the frame. Unfortunately, the 2nd motor was unable to hold the weight of the 2nd arm; thus it starts to fall but the motor tries to return to the position it was meant to be in.
